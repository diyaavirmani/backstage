# Keyword match vs structured evidence

Generated by `npm run eval:structure` from the reviewed research catalog and the production verifier (`scripts/agent-validation.mjs`). The keyword rule marks a requirement established when every key term appears in the venue's published text and any requested count is met by some number in it. Cases are hand-written from real records to probe known failure modes; they are a demonstration, not a general accuracy benchmark.

The comparison changed the product: the first ten cases exposed three over-cautious "unknown" results (a caveat anywhere in a claim had hidden documented facts), and six more exposed two facilities listed inside a hosting claim. Both were fixed. The final six (†) were written after that fix and never tuned on; a later whole-word matching hardening (so "main" cannot match "remain") changed no result. Keyword matching errs toward false "established"; the structured verifier errs toward "not established".
